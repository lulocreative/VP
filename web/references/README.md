## Strategy
- **Manifesto:** Life happens in motion. Participation over sitting it out.
- **The shift:** from "My body works" (functional, claim-led) to "Life feels full of opportunity" (human relevance, freedom, participation).
- **Purpose:** For bodies that still have things to do.
- **Vision:** To become the brand people think of when they choose to keep showing up.
- **Promise:** The life you want to keep living.
- **Personality:** Human · Active · Open · Confident · Playful.
- **Name the enemy (a life getting smaller):** 1 Everyday life → 2 Participation → 3 Mindset ("maybe later" becomes "probably not").
- **Payoff:** Not done yet. The joy of still saying *yes*.

## Tone of voice
*Speak like a person with plans: clear, human and full of life.*
- **Human:** use everyday language. Write as people speak.
- **Grounded:** respect real life, ageing and physical limitations.
- **Confident:** make clear statements. Don't lecture or push.
- **Open:** invite rather than instruct.
- **Playful:** wit is welcome. Never make age, body or limitations the joke.

**Message hierarchy:** Brand storytelling ("Don't live stiff") → Relevance ("Say yes to things you love doing") → Product consideration ("Support for muscles and joints") → Conversion ("Start with improved VitaePro"). Brand storytelling never opens with a discount.

**Campaign architecture:** the *mother campaign* creates demand with no product claims or price. The *child/conversion* campaign captures demand: product, proof and offer lead.

**Guardrails:** every health claim, translation and testimonial passes local compliance review. Don't write "reverse ageing", "eliminate stiffness" or "get your old body back".

## Logo
- The wordmark stands on its own, with **no fixed container**.
- Colour versions: colour on white, reversed on teal. Use black or white only in special circumstances.
- Don't put it in containers, distort it, place it on low-contrast backgrounds or add effects. Vertical use is OK on packaging and merch.
- **Safe space:** at least the width of the ® mark. **Min size:** 160 × 35 px digital, 12 mm wide in print.

## Typography
- **Magister** (headlines): expressive and emotional. Leads stories. Never use it for functional or technical copy.
- **Stratos** (body): functional clarity for body text, product info, UI and labels.
- **Jaqartall** (accent): one or two words only, e.g. *+Gift*, *stiff*, *yes*. Never use it for prices, claims or essential info.
- Reading order: headline (Magister) → body (Stratos) → occasional accent (Jaqartall).

## Graphic assets
- **Organic leaf shape:** based on the VitaePro leaf. Used as the primary leaf, as a price/offer frame (e.g. "First package 9⁹⁰", "-50 %"), and placed or cropped against photos. Always use the approved shape.
- **Container / keyline:** a framing device, not a logo container. Corner radius 50–80 px, 5 px stroke in regular banners, solid or gradient (#D2F1C4 → #FFFFFF). Use it selectively, and never just to frame the wordmark.
- **Quotes:** text overlay, cropped leaf shape (short quote) or speech bubble (longer quote). Credit as "VitaePro Ambassador".
- **Text containers:** fully rounded pills for lists, key points and offers ("Summer offer", "Last chance!", "6 weeks for half price").

## Photography
*The decisive moment: life caught as it happens.*
- Genuine intention, purposeful action, unposed moments. Not a beautiful pose, a beautiful moment.
- **Direct flash:** defined highlights and shadows, keep skin texture, no heavy retouching or stock polish.
- **Composition:** get close, keep it natural, leave room for the message (negative space), capture the action.
- Cast for variety: people aged 45+ to 60+.
- Do: action, expressions, imperfect and spontaneous moments. Don't: generic smiles, forced pack shots, extreme stunts.
- **Storytelling by funnel stage:** Branding = the decisive moment; Relevance/consideration = the afterglow; Conversion = candid, playful interaction with pack or pill.

## Product
- **Pack first:** accurate, recognisable, the sharpest element in product-led images.
- Simple backgrounds and controlled light. The product belongs in life, not just the studio.
- Show the pill, not just the pack. Capture interaction.
