The communication platform

What “Don’t live stiff” means

It’s an attitude towards life.

Not a promise to eliminate physical stiffness. Not a claim that ageing stops. It is a refusal to let routine, hesitation or the challenges of life define who we are.

1Everyday life

When ordinary things start requiring more effort.

2Participation

When it’s easier to sit things out than join in.

3Mindset

When “maybe later” slowly becomes “probably not”.

Name the enemy: the stages of a life that is getting smaller.

The payoff

Not done yet. The joy of still saying yes.

We celebrate the feeling, not the performance. There is no single definition of an active life, no ideal activity level and no one kind of person.

Primary logo

The wordmark stands on its own

The fixed container has been removed. Clear space and sufficient contrast keep the logo clear and legible. Use the version that gives enough contrast. Black and white are for special circumstances only.

Do not make the logo stiff again

No containers

Don’t place the logo inside containers or borders.

Don’t distort

Never stretch, skew or rotate it. Vertical use is fine on packaging and merchandise.

Ensure contrast

Don’t place it on a background where it disappears.

No effects

No shadow, glow or decorative strokes.

Logo safe space

Give the wordmark room to breathe

The more the better. Minimum safe space is the width of the registration mark on every side.

Photography

Not a beautiful pose. A beautiful moment.

Catch life in the act: genuine intention, purposeful action, unposed moments. Direct flash, so it looks immediate, candid and unpolished by design. Cast people aged 45+ to 60+, for variety, not stereotypes.

Product: pack and pills

Make the pack the hero. Then show the pill.

Keep the pack accurate, recognisable and the sharpest element in the frame. Use simple backgrounds and controlled light. The product belongs in life, not just the studio. Give the pill a visible role and capture interaction.
