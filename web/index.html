What “Don’t live stiff” means

It’s an attitude towards life. Not a promise to eliminate physical stiffness. Not a claim that ageing stops. It is a refusal to let routine, hesitation or the challenges of life define who we are.

1 · Everyday life

When ordinary things start requiring more effort.

2 · Participation

When it’s easier to sit things out than join in.

3 · Mindset

When “maybe later” slowly becomes “probably not”.

The payoff

Not done yet. The joy of still saying yes.

We celebrate the feeling, not the performance. There is no single definition of an active life, no ideal activity level and no one kind of person.

Tone of voice

Speak like a person with plans: clear, human and full of life.

Brand book pp. 13–18

Five principles

HumanUse everyday language. Write as people speak, not as brands present themselves.
GroundedRespect real life, ageing and physical limitations.
ConfidentMake clear statements. Avoid lecturing or pushiness.
OpenInvite rather than instruct. Use encouraging language.
PlayfulSurprise and wit are welcome. Never make someone’s age, body or limitations the joke.

The keyline

A framing device, not a logo container. It creates recognition, highlights important messages, builds structure and connects text and image. Use it selectively. It’s not needed in every layout, and never use it only to frame the wordmark.

Visual identity

Photography

The decisive moment: life caught as it happens.

Brand book pp. 46–52

Catch life in the act

Genuine intention

People doing something because they want to, with a clear sense of purpose.

Purposeful action

Show the effort, concentration and what it takes to participate.

Unposed moments

Engaged in what they’re doing, not performing for the viewer.

Direct flash

Immediate. Candid. Unpolished by design. Let the flash create defined highlights and shadows. Keep skin texture and natural imperfections. No excessive smoothing or stock-photo polish.

Tap to fire the flash

Let the action lead

Get close

Tighter framing for expressions, gestures and details.

Natural, not perfect

Don’t ask people to hold a smile or repeat a pose.

Room for the message

Almost every image needs clear negative space for copy.

Capture the action

Movement, interaction and transitions, not props.

Cast for variety, not stereotypes: people aged 45+ to 60+ in different activities.

Capture people. Don’t manufacture happiness.

✓ Do

Action, expressions, gestures, imperfect moments, spontaneous faces and personal stories.

✕ Don’t

Generic smiles, forced pack presentation, extreme stunts or over-directed portraits.
